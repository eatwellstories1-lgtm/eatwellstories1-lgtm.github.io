Frequently asked questions

Is the meal planner really free?

Yes. The PDF planner is a free resource from EatWellStories: you can download it without entering an email. The optional email form adds you to the newsletter waiting list so future guides reach you once the mailing provider is connected.

Is this a medical or weight-loss plan?

No. It provides general balanced-eating ideas for healthy adults and families. It is not a medical treatment, calorie-restricted diet or weight-loss programme. If you have a health condition, food allergy or take medication, ask a doctor or registered dietitian for personalised advice.

Which measurements and ingredients does it use?

Recipes use common UK and US ingredients, with everyday kitchen equipment. Tinned and frozen alternatives are noted so the list works in both countries.

How often will I hear from you?

Only useful, occasional updates: new guides, seasonal meal ideas and the occasional resource update. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email. The site does not sell subscriber data.

Can I print the planner?

Yes. The final page is a blank Monday–Sunday planner grid designed for home printing on A4 or Letter; make copies for each week.
